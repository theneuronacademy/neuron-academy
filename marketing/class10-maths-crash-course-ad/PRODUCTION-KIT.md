## 8. Background music

The rendered film uses an original synthesized bed (`src/music.py`): 100 BPM, C–G–Am–F, soft pad for the hook, plucked arpeggio from 5 s, kick and hi-hat from 14.4 s. It contains no third-party audio, so there is no licence to clear.

To replace it with a fuller commercial track, use `output/…-no-music.mp4` and add one of these types, keeping the music 14–18 dB below the voice:
- YouTube Audio Library – filter Genre: *Dance & Electronic* or *Pop*, Mood: *Inspirational* or *Bright* (free to use; check each track's attribution note).
- Pixabay Music – search "corporate upbeat technology" or "inspiring education" (check the licence shown on the track page).
- Instagram's own music library is the safest choice for a Reel posted from a personal or creator account; business accounts have a restricted library.

Look for 95–110 BPM, a soft 4–5 s intro and a clear lift around 15 s.
