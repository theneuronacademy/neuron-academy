**Mathematics on screen (checked)**

- sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 (right triangle with hypotenuse 1, base cos θ, perpendicular sin θ).
- Dividing every term by cos²θ: sin²θ/cos²θ + cos²θ/cos²θ = 1/cos²θ, so tan²θ + 1 = sec²θ.
- Dividing every term by sin²θ: sin²θ/sin²θ + cos²θ/sin²θ = 1/sin²θ, so 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ.
- Trap: sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 is correct; tan²θ − sec²θ = 1 is wrong (it equals −1).
- The divisions need cos θ ≠ 0 and sin θ ≠ 0 respectively; for the acute angles of Class 10 the second identity holds for 0° ≤ θ < 90° and the third for 0° < θ ≤ 90°, as NCERT states. The reel does not show these conditions; mention them in class.

## Strategy

Main problem: students memorise three identities separately and mix up the signs. Unique value: one identity to remember, two divisions to get the rest, and the one sign error that costs marks.
