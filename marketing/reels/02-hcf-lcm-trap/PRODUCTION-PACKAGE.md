**Mathematics on screen (all checked by calculation)**

- 6 and 20: HCF = 2, LCM = 60. HCF × LCM = 2 × 60 = 120 = 6 × 20.
- For any two positive integers a and b: HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b.
- 6 = 2 × 3, 72 = 2³ × 3², 120 = 2³ × 3 × 5. HCF = 2 × 3 = 6. LCM = 2³ × 3² × 5 = 360.
- HCF × LCM = 6 × 360 = 2,160, but 6 × 72 × 120 = 51,840. Not equal.
- Both number sets are worked examples in the NCERT chapter. They are not labelled as PYQs.
